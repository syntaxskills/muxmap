PAYMENT INFRASTRUCTURE / PRODUCT STRATEGY

Brands pay once. Creators
get paid directly.

Payment infrastructure for a creator marketplace in Singapore and Southeast Asia. We compare Stripe and Airwallex, with one key requirement: creators provide bank details or a PayNow number and do not need an external wallet account.

Project: Viral AsiaMarkets: SG · MY · TH · ID · PH · VNUpdated: 20 October 2026Type: Product and commercial decision brief
RECOMMENDED APPROACH

Collect with Stripe. Pay with Airwallex.

If Viral Asia sells the advertising service, this is the simplest path to test first. Keep Stripe for brand payments. Use the Airwallex API to pay creators into their bank accounts across Southeast Asia.

CREATOR EXPERIENCE

No PayPal account needed

Creators enter payout details inside Viral Asia. Identity checks and bank fields still depend on local rules and the payment product.

MAIN STRIPE LIMIT

Cross-border payouts from Singapore are limited

Singapore is not on Stripe Connect’s published list for self-service cross-border payouts. Stripe Global Payouts does not currently list Singapore as a sending country.

01 Objective

Collect brand payments and pay many creators

All actions should stay inside Viral Asia. A brand pays for a campaign. The platform records the work, approves delivery, and then pays each creator.

✓
Accept online brand paymentsCards, Apple Pay and PayNow, subject to the provider and account setup.
✓
Retain a platform commissionRecord fees by order, campaign, or the agreed creator rate.
✓
Only ask creators for payout detailsNo need to redirect creators to PayPal or another wallet.
✓
Automate cross-border payoutsPrefer PayNow in Singapore and local bank transfers in other markets.
Important distinction:You can avoid an external app sign-up. You cannot promise that a phone number alone is enough in every case. A provider may ask for ID, address, or bank details based on the country, amount, and risk.
02 Decision

Stripe is strong for payment collection and local Connect payouts in Singapore. But its published, self-service Connect cross-border coverage does not include Singapore-based platforms. Global Payouts also does not list Singapore as an eligible sending country.[3][4]

04 Architecture

Recommended end-to-end payment flow

For the first release, implement “brand settlement + batch payouts after delivery approval”. Do not pay creators as soon as a brand payment webhook arrives.

STEP 01
Brand paysStripe Checkout / PayNow
STEP 02
Verify settlementWebhook + actual bank settlement
STEP 03
Approve deliveryPlatform confirms the work
STEP 04
Initiate payoutAirwallex Payouts API
STEP 05
Confirm and reconcilePayout status / retry failures
Money flow: Stripe settles to the company’s designated account → funds reach the available Airwallex balance → approved creator payouts are sent. Moving funds between providers is not an instant internal transfer. Allow for settlement time and working capital.

What backend records are required?

Campaign(campaign) → BrandPayment(brand payment) → CreatorAssignment(creator work and amount) → Beneficiary(beneficiary data) → Payout(transfer and status)

Required controls: webhook signature checks, idempotency keys, duplicate-payment prevention, approval logs, safe retries, refunds and chargebacks, data encryption, and least-privilege access.

06 Risk & regulation

Define the contract roles before choosing the fund flow

BUSINESS MODEL A

Viral Asia is the service provider

The brand buys a complete advertising service from Viral Asia. Viral Asia is responsible for delivery. Creators are contracted suppliers.

Start by assessing normal business collections and supplier payments. Have legal counsel review contracts, tax, and fund ownership.

BUSINESS MODEL B

Viral Asia is only an agent or intermediary

Brands contract creators directly. Viral Asia collects or manages funds and charges a commission.

Assess a dedicated Marketplace or managed-split-payments product. Review Singapore Payment Services Act duties.

Important:Singapore’s Payment Services Act regulates certain domestic transfers, cross-border transfers, and merchant acquisition services. Whether a licence or exemption is needed depends on the contracts and actual business activity. Using a licensed provider does not remove the need for this review.[11]

Resolve these points before launch

Write down who owns the funds, when payouts may be held, who bears chargeback losses, what creator ID is required, whether account names must match, country tax reporting, invoicing, and refund rules. Confirm the final flow with the payment provider.

07 Next steps

Minimum viable launch plan

01
Confirm service contracts and fund ownership

Check whether the brand buys Viral Asia’s service or buys directly from creators. This decision determines the payment design.

02
Get Airwallex approval for Marketplace / Payouts

Confirm the Singapore company, individual beneficiaries in SG/MY/TH/ID/PH/VN, PayNow/DuitNow phone routing, per-payout fees, FX, KYC, and release timing.

03
Build a creator payout-settings page

Show only the fields needed for each country. Collect legal names and any required extra checks. Do not require a PayPal account.

04
Test small real payouts first

Pilot SG PayNow, MY DuitNow, and local bank transfers to Thailand and Indonesia. Check name matching, failure reasons, actual fees, and arrival time.

05
Automate reconciliation and batch settlement

After approvals, risk checks, and refund logic work, add status webhooks, idempotent processing, and finance reports.
